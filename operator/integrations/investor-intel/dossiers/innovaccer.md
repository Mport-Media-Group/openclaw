## Strategic posture

- Acquisition appetite: **High and active.** 5 acquisitions to date; CaduceusHealth was the most recent (announced 2026-05-21, ~$66M per Axios Pro). Pattern is bolt-on AI workflow companies adjacent to their data platform.
- Partnership entry points: Their Flow suite for ambulatory care is the platform side; the post-acute/discharge layer is the gap they have NOT solved. Possible partnership entry rather than competitive head-on.
- Competitive threat level: **Medium-low** at DCB's current focus (discharge → post-acute coordination). High overlap with Innovaccer if DCB ever expanded into ambulatory RCM, scheduling, or population-health analytics — those are their core.
- Past behavior pattern: They name targets, raise post-acquisition press cycles around "agentic" and "autonomous" framing, and integrate into the Flow / Health Cloud platform brand. They DO NOT seem to leave acquired brands standalone.

## Verified facts (CaduceusHealth deal)

- Announced: **2026-05-21** (asset acquisition; deal value ~**$66M** per Axios Pro)
